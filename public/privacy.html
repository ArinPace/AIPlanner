Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · WHOOP integration and related planning data

This policy explains how AI Planner handles data you authorize through WHOOP and how that information may be used in your calendar, planning tools and assistant conversations.

Who is responsible

AI Planner is operated by its developer. For privacy questions, data access, correction or deletion requests, contact jainarnav364@gmail.com. Please identify the Planner account concerned; we may need to verify ownership before acting on a request. Do not email passwords or access tokens.

Information you choose to connect

Connecting WHOOP is optional. With your consent, AI Planner requests read-only access to the WHOOP API categories below, including available historical records:

  • Profile: WHOOP user identifier, name and email address.
  • Body measurements: height, weight and maximum heart rate.
  • Sleep: recorded sleep times, stages, duration, performance and other sleep metrics supplied by WHOOP.
  • Recovery: recovery scores and available measurements such as resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen and skin temperature.
  • Physiological cycles: cycle times, strain, energy expenditure, heart-rate metrics and available step counts.
  • Workouts: activity type, times, duration, strain, heart-rate zones and other workout fields supplied by WHOOP.

Available fields depend on WHOOP, your device, membership and recorded data. Access to these categories does not mean WHOOP exposes every measurement, journal entry or raw sensor stream in its consumer app. Planner also processes your account details, calendar, tasks, notes, assistant messages and other sources you independently choose to connect.

How we receive and use data

We use connected information to provide the features you request: answer questions, inform scheduling and recovery decisions, prepare calendar changes and maintain your planning context. WHOOP notifications alert our cloud service to sleep, recovery and workout changes; the service then fetches authorized records. Quiet reconciliation and on-demand reads can refresh other categories or retrieve history. Receiving a notification does not by itself publish a calendar change.

WHOOP authorization and refresh tokens let Planner maintain access until you disconnect or authorization expires. WHOOP sign-in occurs through WHOOP; this integration does not ask you to provide your WHOOP password to Planner. We use essential browser state, including a short-lived OAuth cookie, to verify connection attempts. Web requests may generate operational logs such as request times, route and error information.

We do not sell WHOOP data or use it for advertising, and we do not train our own AI models on it. AI Planner is a planning tool, not a medical diagnosis or treatment service.

Service providers and assistant processing

AI Planner uses Vercel for hosting and server execution and Supabase for database storage. Relevant WHOOP information may be provided to the AI service you use for a requested answer or enabled review, such as ChatGPT/OpenAI or another configured assistant provider. Those providers process information under their own terms, privacy policies and your account settings; this policy does not promise a particular retention or training setting for an independent provider.

If you enable calendar publishing, relevant planning information may also be written to your connected calendar provider. Health-related details can consequently appear in calendar entries, notes or chat history. Other people may be able to see material you separately share through those services. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect the security of the service.

Storage, security and retention

Authorization tokens are encrypted before storage. WHOOP records are stored in server-restricted, account-scoped cloud storage, and web connections use HTTPS. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cached WHOOP records and profile snapshots may remain while your connection is active. Successfully processed webhook receipts are eligible for cleanup after 30 days during scheduled maintenance; unresolved receipts can remain until processing succeeds or you disconnect. Messages, notes and calendar entries containing information already used for planning have their own lifecycle and are not automatically erased by disconnecting WHOOP. Infrastructure backups and independent service providers may retain copies under their own retention practices.

Your choices, disconnection and deletion

Open Add sites → Health → WHOOP → Disconnect WHOOP to revoke the integration and delete its active stored authorization, cached WHOOP records, profile snapshots and webhook receipts. You may also revoke access in WHOOP. Revoking access only in WHOOP stops future authorized access but does not itself delete previously stored Planner data.

To request access to or deletion of remaining information, including WHOOP-derived content in Planner chats, notes or calendar entries, email jainarnav364@gmail.com. Deleting Planner's copies does not automatically delete copies already sent to independent AI, calendar or other services; use their controls or contact them separately. Any applicable legal rights depend on your location and circumstances.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the integration or its data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. New permissions require the appropriate authorization.
